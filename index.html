About

I'm br413 — a Senior Data Engineer and Data Architect who turns fragmented sources into reliable, observable, analytics-ready platforms. Senior data engineering, to me, is less about tool count and more about judgment under constraint: choosing trade-offs intentionally, designing for failure before the first incident, and keeping systems understandable for the team that inherits them.

My portfolio connects a runnable lakehouse reference (Iceberg, Trino, Cosmos, OpenLineage), an ETL reference pipeline with Airflow and dbt, CSV data contract checks, and platform IaC blueprints. These are demonstrations, not evidence of client deployments, production scale, or customer outcomes. Prior employer work lived in private GitLab/Azure DevOps; this portfolio is not a full employment history.

Data platform maintenance

For teams seeking ongoing ownership, the maintenance work I can discuss includes:

  • Pipeline reliability: failed-run investigation, ingestion and dbt repairs, recovery, backfills, and recurring-failure fixes.
  • Quality and observability: data contract and schema checks, run-history review, and actionable alerts.

We begin by agreeing on the platform, ownership boundaries, backlog, and support expectations. Scope and response arrangements are agreed per engagement.
